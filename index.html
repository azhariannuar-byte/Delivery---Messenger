Privacy and sending

This app does not send messages automatically, access your contacts, or store recipient numbers. It prepares the message and opens WhatsApp or your SMS app. You review the recipient and press Send yourself. Contact customers only where permitted by your delivery platform and the recipient's communication preferences. Repeated unsolicited messages can still lead to spam reports or account restrictions.
