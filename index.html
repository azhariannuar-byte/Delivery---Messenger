Privacy and sending

This app does not access your contacts or send messages automatically. It prepares a message and opens WhatsApp or SMS. Check the recipient and message, then press Send yourself. Contact customers only where permitted by the delivery platform and the recipient's communication preferences. Unsolicited messages can lead to spam reports or account restrictions.
